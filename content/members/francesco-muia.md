Francesco Muia is a data scientist who applies Bayesian modeling and simulation-based inference to research in quantitative finance for a hedge fund. Alongside this work, he contributes to [HSSM](https://lnccbrown.github.io/HSSM/), a toolbox for Bayesian cognitive modeling. Before moving to data science, he worked as a theoretical physicist specializing in string theory and cosmology.

- **2024 – present** – Independent Bayesian data scientist
- **2022 – 2024** – Executive MBA, SDA Bocconi
- **2020 – 2024** – Stephen Hawking Fellow, University of Cambridge (DAMTP)
- **2019 – 2020** – Postdoctoral Researcher, DESY Hamburg
- **2017 – 2019** – Postdoctoral Researcher, ICTP Trieste
- **2016 – 2017** – Postdoctoral Researcher, University of Oxford
- **2013 – 2016** – Ph.D., University of Bologna (Theoretical Physics)
